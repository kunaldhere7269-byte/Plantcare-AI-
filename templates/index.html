Capabilities

Comprehensive Agricultural Intelligence

Designed to address real farming and gardening challenges through transparent AI, safe integrated pest management, and continuous plant observation.

Plant Disease Detection

Diagnoses fungal, bacterial, and pest-induced leaf anomalies like Early Blight, Powdery Mildew, Rust, and Leaf Spots with photographic evidence analysis.

  • Detects chlorosis, necrosis, and target rings
  • Multi-level severity rating (Low, Moderate, High)
  • Real confidence scoring & uncertainty flags

Personalized Growth Care

Generates practical guidance tailored to plant species, growing location, and user-reported watering habits to optimize photosynthesis and yield.

  • Soil drainage & balanced watering tips
  • Photoperiod & sunlight requirements
  • Organic nutrient feeding recommendations

Eco-Friendly IPM Treatments

Prioritizes Integrated Pest Management (IPM), cultural sanitation, and bio-fungicides to protect local ecosystems and avoid chemical pesticide resistance.

  • Immediate non-toxic containment steps
  • Organic neem oil and bio-agent guidelines
  • Strict chemical PPE & safety precautions

Longitudinal Progress Log

Stores every monitoring session in a permanent SQLite database, allowing growers to track disease progression or recovery across seasons.

  • Side-by-side scan comparison tool
  • Customizable observation notes
  • Fast search and status filtering

Honest Vision Quality Check

Uses OpenCV to measure blur variance, exposure levels, and chlorophyll presence so the system never hallucinates diagnoses on non-plant photos.

  • Blur detection via Laplacian variance
  • Chlorosis / necrosis pixel quantification
  • Non-plant image guardrails

Dynamic Analytics Dashboard

Visualizes health trends, common crop vulnerabilities, and severity distributions with interactive Chart.js graphs computed live from records.

  • Health status distribution chart
  • Top vulnerable crop species tracking
  • Seasonal garden tips carousel
Simple Workflow

How PlantCare AI Operates

A clear, accessible 3-step process built for both rural farmers on mobile phones and students in university labs.

1
Snap a Clear Leaf Photo

Upload a well-lit photo of the affected plant leaf. Optionally provide plant species, watering frequency, and symptoms.

2
Computer Vision & AI Scan

The image is analyzed using multimodal vision AI (Gemini Vision API) or transparent heuristic pathology rules if offline.

3
Apply Actionable Guidance

Receive instant IPM treatment options, preventive measures, personalized growth advice, and save to your plant health journal.
